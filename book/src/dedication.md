# Dedication

<p style="margin-top: 4em; font-size: 1.25em; text-align: center;"><em>For Fred Espenak</em></p>

<p style="text-align: center;">who computed where the Moon's shadow falls<br>
and gave the results to everyone who wanted to stand in it.</p>

<p style="margin-top: 4em; font-size: 0.875em; text-align: center;">
Every eclipse in these pages is held against the <em>Five Millennium Canons</em> he wrote with
Jean Meeus. He had no part in this work, and its errors are its own.</p>
