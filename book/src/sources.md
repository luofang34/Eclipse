# Sources

## The Canons

Espenak, F. and Meeus, J., *Five Millennium Canon of Solar Eclipses: -1999 to +3000*, NASA
Technical Publication TP-2006-214141.

Espenak, F. and Meeus, J., *Five Millennium Canon of Lunar Eclipses: -1999 to +3000*, NASA
Technical Publication TP-2009-214172.

## Methods

*Explanatory Supplement to the Astronomical Almanac*, for the fundamental plane and the shadow
cones.

Meeus, J., *Astronomical Algorithms*, chapter 49, for the mean new moon of lunation 0.

## The Earth's rotation

Stephenson, F. R., Morrison, L. V. and Hohenkerk, C. Y., "Measurement of the Earth's rotation:
720 BC to AD 2015", *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 472 (2016).

## Records

Halley, E., on the eclipse of 1715, *Philosophical Transactions* 29 (1714-1716).

Herodotus, *Histories* 1.74, in Rawlinson's translation.

Stephenson, F. R. and Steele, J. M., *Journal for the History of Astronomy* 37 (2006), for the
Babylonian record of -135.

## Software

[Navigate](https://github.com/luofang34/Navigate) for the Sun, the Moon, the stars and ΔT.

[The MapLibre fork](https://github.com/luofang34/maplibre-rs-experimental) for the globe and
the sky.

[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) for coastlines and borders.
